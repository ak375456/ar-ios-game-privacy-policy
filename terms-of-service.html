03Play safely in AR

Important

Drive AR puts a virtual car into your real surroundings. While you watch the screen, it is easy to forget what is around you. You are responsible for playing in a safe place and in a safe way.

  • Clear a space first. Check for stairs, cables, toys, pets, furniture edges and anything fragile.
  • Look up from the screen often, and be careful when walking backwards or sideways.
  • Never play while driving, cycling or crossing a road, or near traffic, water, balconies, platforms or other drops.
  • Only play where you are allowed to be. Respect other people’s property and privacy.
  • Children should play with an adult nearby.
  • Take regular breaks. Stop playing if you feel dizzy, sick or have eye strain, or any discomfort.
  • Photosensitivity: a very small number of people may have seizures triggered by flashing lights or patterns, even with no history of epilepsy. If you or anyone in your family has had a seizure, ask a doctor before playing. Stop immediately if you have any unusual symptoms.
  • The car is a toy simulation. It is not a driving lesson and does not show how real vehicles behave.
