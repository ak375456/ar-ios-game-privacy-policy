01Who we are

Drift Anywhere: AR Cars (“the game” or “the app”) is an augmented-reality driving game for iPhone made by Aftab Fazal Qayum, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). On your iPhone’s Home Screen and in Settings it appears as Drift Anywhere. For the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), Aftab Fazal Qayum is the controller of the small amount of personal data described in section 5.

Contact: ak375456@gmail.com

02What we collect

The short version

The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no sign-in, and it goes online only through Apple’s in-app purchase system, to show prices and to buy or restore purchases. On the App Store, its privacy label is “Data Not Collected.”

Specifically, the app does not have or use:

  • accounts, sign-in, names, email addresses or phone numbers;
  • advertising, ad networks or advertising identifiers (IDFA) — and it never asks to track you;
  • analytics, crash-reporting or attribution software development kits (SDKs) from third parties;
  • location services, contacts, the microphone or health data;
  • your payment details — in-app purchases are paid through Apple, and we never see your card or billing information;
  • its own servers. Apart from Apple’s in-app purchase system, the game does not connect to the internet.

We do not sell or share personal information, we do not use it for targeted advertising, and we do not make automated decisions or build profiles about you.

03What stays on your iPhone

To work, the game uses some features of your iPhone. Everything below is processed on your device only. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

  • CameraShows your room and lets Apple’s ARKit find the floor so the car can drive on it. Camera frames are analysed live and never saved or sent anywhere. On device
  • Room and people occlusionOn supported iPhones, ARKit can work out the shape of the room (with LiDAR) or where people stand, so the car can hide behind them. This happens in real time and is discarded when you leave AR. On device
  • Photos (add only)Photos and videos you choose to capture are saved to your photo library. The app asks only for “Add Photos Only” access, so it cannot see your existing photos. On device
  • Screen recordingGameplay videos use Apple’s ReplayKit. The microphone is turned off, so videos contain no sound from your surroundings. iOS asks your permission before recording starts. On device
  • Game progress and settingsCoins, stars, cars, upgrades, paint, missions and your control layout are saved in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. On device
  • In-app purchasesApple’s StoreKit tells the game, on your iPhone, which items you have bought so it can unlock them. Apple takes the payment; the game only learns which item you bought. On device
  • Date and timersThe device date unlocks daily missions and the daily coin bonus. A system timer measures things like drift time. Neither is used to identify you. On device

You can turn camera or photo access off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your iPhone. If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, iOS may include the game’s progress in that backup; Apple manages backups under its own privacy policy, and we cannot access them.

04Information from Apple

Apple distributes the game through the App Store and processes data such as your Apple Account and download history under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive that information.

If you have turned on Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Share With App Developers, Apple may give us crash reports and aggregate statistics, such as the number of installs. This information does not identify you. We use it only to fix bugs and improve the game. You can turn this off at any time.

In-app purchases

When you buy something in the game, Apple processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your name, Apple Account, email address, card or billing details. Apple gives us sales reports showing, for example, which item was sold, in which country, and how much we earned. These reports do not identify you, and we use them only for accounting, tax and improving the game.

05When you email support

The only personal data we ever receive is what you choose to send us by email. If you write to us, we receive:

  • your email address and the name shown on your email account;
  • your message and anything you attach, such as screenshots or your iPhone model and iOS version.

We use this only to reply to you and fix the problem you report. We never add you to a mailing list, use it for marketing, or share it with anyone, except where the law requires it.

Legal basis (GDPR / UK GDPR). Answering your request (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in supporting players and improving the game (Art. 6(1)(f)). Switzerland (FADP). We process this data in line with the principles in Art. 6 FADP: only for the purpose you contacted us about, and only for as long as needed.

Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), which stores messages for us as a service provider under the Google Privacy Policy.

07Children

The game is for a general audience. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) or under the age of digital consent in their country (13 to 16 in the EU and UK).

Parents can stop or approve purchases with Apple’s Screen Time and Ask to Buy features. See Support for how.

If you believe a child has emailed us, a parent or guardian can contact us and we will delete the message.
