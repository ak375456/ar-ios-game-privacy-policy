FAQQuick fixes

The car won’t place, or the floor isn’t found
  1. Turn on the lights. AR needs a well-lit room.
  2. Point the camera at the floor about 1–2 metres in front of you.
  3. Move your iPhone slowly from side to side for a few seconds, so ARKit can map the floor.
  4. Floors with some texture, such as wood, tiles or rugs, work best. Plain white, very shiny, glass or very dark floors are harder to detect.
  5. When the reticle appears on the floor, tap to place the car.

Still stuck? Go back to the garage and press Drive again to start a fresh AR session.

The camera is black or access was denied

Drive AR needs the camera to show your room. Open Settings › Apps › Drive AR (on older iOS versions, Settings › Drive AR) and turn on Camera. Then return to the game.

The camera image is processed only on your iPhone. It is never saved or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.

Photos or videos aren’t saving

Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Drive AR and choose Add Photos Only. The game can then save your captures, but it still can’t see your existing photos.

To capture, pause the game and open Tools. Videos are recorded without the microphone, so they have no sound from your room. If iOS asks for permission to record the screen, tap Record Screen.

The car slides, jumps or floats

This usually means AR tracking was lost for a moment.

  • Keep the camera uncovered and move your iPhone smoothly, not quickly.
  • Add more light, and avoid pointing the camera at blank walls or mirrors.
  • Tap Reset to put the car back where you placed it, or pause and choose Move car to place it somewhere else.
I want to move or resize the controls

In the garage, tap the settings button at the top right to open Controls. You can rearrange and resize the controls, and change the transmission, speed units (km/h or mph), instruments, haptics and tyre-smoke effects. Reset restores the default layout.

For a clean shot, pause and choose Clean view to hide the controls. Tap the eye button to bring them back.

Do I need internet or an account?

You don’t need an account, and you can play offline. You only need the internet to buy something or restore a purchase, because Apple handles the payment. There are no ads, and the game sends nothing to us.

How do I get coins and cars?

Earn coins by picking up road coins while you drive, completing missions, and with the daily bonus. Stars come from missions. Use both to unlock the next car in the Collection.

You can also buy some items in the game. Prices are shown in your local currency before you confirm, and Apple takes the payment. Read Coins, cars and purchases in the Terms.

How do I restore my purchases?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy, connect to the internet, and tap Restore Purchases in the game.

This brings back permanent unlocks. Coins and other items that get used up are saved only on your iPhone, so they can’t be restored. They come back only if you restore your iPhone from a backup that contains them.

I paid but didn’t get my item
  1. Check that Apple charged you. Look for Apple’s receipt email, or open Settings › [your name] › Media & Purchases › View Account › Purchase History.
  2. Make sure you’re online, then close Drive AR completely and open it again. Purchases that didn’t finish are delivered when the game starts.
  3. For a permanent unlock, tap Restore Purchases.
  4. Still missing? Email us with the order ID from Apple’s receipt and we’ll sort it out.
How do I get a refund?

Apple handles every payment, so only Apple can refund a purchase. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose Request a refund, and pick the item.

This is also where to cancel a purchase if your country gives you a right to do so, as in the EU and UK.

How do I stop my child from buying things?
  • Block purchases: open Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions, turn it on, then set In-app Purchases to Don’t Allow.
  • Approve each purchase: with Family Sharing, turn on Ask to Buy for your child, so every purchase needs your OK.

If your child bought something without permission, request a refund from Apple (see the question above).

Will I lose my progress?

Your progress is saved only on your iPhone. Updates keep it, but deleting the app deletes your progress, including coins you bought and haven’t spent. We keep no copy to restore it. Permanent unlocks can be restored with Restore Purchases.

When you set up a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one, your progress usually comes with it.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone running iOS 18 or later that supports ARKit world tracking. LiDAR isn’t needed. On iPhones with LiDAR (Pro models), the game can also scan the room so the car hides behind real furniture.

What data do you collect? How do I delete it?

The app collects no personal data. Everything stays on your iPhone, and deleting the app removes it. If you have emailed us and want that conversation deleted, just ask. Read the full Privacy Policy, which covers the GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss FADP and CCPA.
